Acme Industrial Corp. second-quarter commentary

Acme reported second-quarter revenue of $4.2 billion, up 18% year over year and ahead of the $3.9 billion consensus. Operating margin expanded to 24.5%, the highest level in the company's history, helped by a favorable product mix and lower freight costs. Management raised full-year guidance and the board approved a 15% increase in the quarterly dividend.

The quarter was not without problems. Supply chain disruptions forced Acme to halt production at its Ohio plant for two weeks in May, and management warned that component shortages could persist into the fourth quarter. The company also disclosed that regulators have opened an investigation into pricing practices in its European distribution unit; no provision has been recorded.

Net debt fell to $1.1 billion after the company repaid a $500 million term loan early. Free cash flow was $610 million for the quarter. Management will host a conference call at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time on Thursday.
